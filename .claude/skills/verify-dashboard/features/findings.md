# Findings

## Sub-features
- `/findings` (customer_admin and viewer), heading "Findings": two tabs, "Prioritized list" first, then "Manual closures" (`?tab=closed`). The legacy `?tab=summary|before-after|risk|threats` redirect to the Summary (`/dashboard`) and follow its mapping
- `/dashboard` (sidebar "Summary"), heading "Summary": the inventory sync line ("Inventory synced <time>" with a green dot; with a red dot "The last inventory sync failed <time>. These numbers are from <date>." when the failure is newer than the last sync; nothing with no CMDB Source), then two tabs: "Overview" (default, `/dashboard`) and "Prioritization and threats" (`?tab=analysis`). `?tab=before-after|threats` land on `?tab=analysis`, `?tab=closed` on `/findings?tab=closed`, any other `tab` on `/dashboard`
- Overview, with no "?" help: "Open right now" with "↓ N since last Monday" (green), "↑ N since last Monday" (red) or "No change since last Monday" against last week's `open_at_end`; "Critical, open right now" in the Critical colour with "N of them are on internet-facing assets", or "None" in navy; the open backlog as one teal line from the first week with findings, no axes, labelled at the first week, the highest and now, titled "The open backlog is shrinking|growing|holding steady" (first week against now), or "The trend appears after your second week." under 2 weeks; "Start here": the first two groups with "N findings, K of them Critical" and "Open the prioritized list" (the `findings.empty` message with no prioritization); "Coming back soon": closes by hand whose reopen day falls in the next 30 days, soonest first, at most 3, "<CVE> on <asset>" over "<Accepted risk|Postponed> by <email> · back <date>", then "N more"; "Nothing set aside comes back in the next 30 days." with none
- Prioritized list: the current order of open Findings, kept by the Prioritizer, under a filter bar (search, Band, Exposure, Status, KEV only; filters live in the URL, with chips and "Clear all")
- Summary line: "Updated <date> · N findings, M groups · Weighed with business context vN" (or "without a business context"); "No findings yet. ..." replaces the list when there is no prioritization
- One panel per group: "Group N" badge, the group title and reason (written by the Prioritizer), a table (cards below 768px, see phone-cards.md) CVE / Asset / Component / Score (0-100 plus a band badge Critical/High/Medium/Low) / By Score (#rank) / Status badges (Closed, KEV, Ransomware, New, Plan)
- Clicking a row or "Open <CVE>" opens the detail modal; the open finding is in the URL (`?finding=<id>`), so a reload or a link lands on it
- Detail: a rail (CVE, component, asset, Score, rank, badges, CISA KEV, EPSS, CVSS, Exposure, Criticality, Lifecycle Stage, Information Classification, placed date) beside a column that reads Why it is here (the group reason, cited values marked), the "Is it in the right place?" box, Action plan, then folded sections that unfold on a click: How it would be attacked, Vulnerability (description, "CVSS <version> vector" of the highest CVSS, CISA's "Required action" unless the plan's "Until it is fixed" says it), Asset (every attribute, component and role, critical services), Score (factors and multipliers; "Estimated" with gap badges only when inputs were missing), Vendor advisories, References (only those the plan does not cite, and only when there are any). Nothing in the column repeats another part of it; the rail is the summary
- No visible text names an agent (Luna, the Prioritizer, the Cartógrafo, the Editor, the Analista), says something was inferred, or shows the Scoring Model
- Action plan: "Get an action plan" / "Ask for a new plan", "Writing the plan. It can take a minute…", then "Written <date>", summary, "Steps", "Version that fixes it" (narrow) beside "How to verify" (wide), "Until it is fixed", "MITRE mitigations for this asset", "Sources"
- "Is it in the right place?": three verdict buttons (✓ It applies, ↑ Should be attended earlier, ↓ Should be attended later, `aria-pressed` on the chosen one); choosing one shows a required "Why" with a "N / 2000" count and "Send" (disabled while Why is blank), then "Saved."; below, "What was said (N)" lists the verdicts (older ones may say "It does not apply" or "Already remediated")
- Prioritization history (ADR 0036): the detail fetches `GET /findings/{id}/history` when it opens and shows it in the folded "Prioritization history <count>" section, right under Score; "Why it is here" says nothing of the moves. Each entry, newest first: a mark (↑ ↓ ● ○), the headline (Entered the order in Group N, Moved up/down from Group X to Y, Looked at again; still in Group N, Left the priority order), "Group N · <title>", what changed (placed_again, a round that read the Finding again, even if it kept its group: KEV, ransomware, ATT&CK, Band, Score ×100, each user's earlier/later note, business context, or "Looked at again with no identifiable change"; moved: "The finding did not change; the groups above it did." and "New above it" / "No longer above it"), a comparison "[Score N or A → B ·] By Score alone #R · In the prioritization #F–#T" (the range its group takes in the order, "#F" for a group of one; the Score left out when "What changed" already says it moved) with a badge "Ahead of the Score" (R past the range), "Behind the Score" (R before it); none when R falls inside it; left entries instead say "It was at: Group N · #F–#T · Score S" and, only the newest while still closed, "Closed: <reason>" (a manual reason or a Sync one: No longer vulnerable, The component was removed, The asset is no longer active, The vulnerability was rejected), and "Why it was placed there" for every entry but the newest (its reason is the one in "Why it is here"). Loading is a skeleton, a failure says so with Retry inside the section only, empty says "It is not in the priority order yet." Refetched after a close, a reopen and Feedback. The mock tells three stories (CVE-2024-23897, CVE-2021-41773, CVE-2025-1094); any other Finding has one "placed" entry from the oldest round that holds it, and a closed one gets a "left" entry at once
- Close by hand (ADR 0035, Customer Admin and Viewer): "Close finding", filled, alone in the detail's top bar (a divider sets it apart from the ✕ "Close" of the modal; offered nowhere else) opens "Close <CVE>": a Reason radio group (Already remediated, False positive / does not apply, Accepted risk, Postponed), each with what happens next; "Reopens after" with "In 30 days" / "In 90 days" only for Accepted risk and Postponed; a required Why. Once closed: "Closed" badge and a dimmed row in the list, Open count down by one, the detail's rail shows the reason, the note, "Closed by <email> on <date>" and "Reopens after <date>", and the top bar offers "Reopen" (confirm "Reopen <CVE>?", "Yes, reopen"). A Finding closed on its own says "This finding closed automatically; ..." and offers neither
- The Score Band is called "Risk level" on screen ("Nivel de riesgo"; "No risk level" / "Sin nivel"): the filter, its chip, the history and every chart. Its colours: Critical red, High orange, Medium yellow, Low gray, No risk level light gray; the Before & After composition has a legend of the levels they show
- Prioritization and threats (`/dashboard?tab=analysis`): the "Before & After" section, then the "Threats" section, each under its heading; their cards keep their "?" help
- Threats: coverage, then "Findings per tactic" with every Technique role together (no filter): a column only for the tactics some finding leads to, in ATT&CK order, and "No findings: <tactics>" under it
- Before & After: the newest prioritization against the order by Score alone (Findings sorted by Score rank, cut into groups the size of the real ones): Went up / Stayed / Went down / Match counts, the movement matrix (score group rows, priority group columns, a cell filters the list to its Findings), "Most significant movements" (CVE as "Open <CVE>", Component, By score, By priority, Δ; no reason: the detail tells it) where a row opens the Finding's detail over the tab and closing it comes back, and the band composition per group
- Closed tab (`?tab=closed`): the Findings closed by hand, newest first: CVE, Asset · Component, Reason badge, Why, Closed (who, when), Reopens on (a date, or "Will reopen at the next sync" once it passed), "Reopen <CVE>" per row; "No findings closed by hand" when empty; cards below 768px

## How to get to it (user POV)
Sidebar link "Findings"; the Summary is the sidebar's "Summary" (customer_admin and viewer land on it).

## Driving it with Playwright
`node drive.mjs summary`: connect iTop, then the Overview: the green sync line, "↑ 2 since last Monday" (23 open, 21 at last week's end), "3 of them are on internet-facing assets", "The open backlog is growing" labelled 12 and 23, Start here ("4 findings, 2 of them Critical"), Coming back soon empty, no "?" button; checks no call went to the retired chart endpoints. Then a failed Sync seeded into `barbattack_cmdb_source` shows the red line.

`node drive.mjs before-after`: the second tab in `?tab=analysis`, the movements without a Reason column, a row's detail over the tab and back, the tactics matrix with only its "?" button, and `?tab=before-after` landing on the tab.

`node drive.mjs finding-history`: open CVE-2024-23897, "Why it is here" tells no moves, unfold "Prioritization history" (under Score): "Moved down from Group 1 to 2" with "New above it: …", "Score 19 · By Score alone #17 · In the prioritization #5–#9" with "Ahead of the Score", then the climb with KEV, Band, Score and a user's feedback, and an older reason on demand; then CVE-2025-1094 shows "Looked at again; still in Group 2". Checks every history call was answered 200.

`node drive.mjs finding-closes`: as a Viewer, the three verdict buttons, no Why until one is chosen and then a disabled Send, a single "Close finding" in the top bar, Postponed in 30 days with a Why, the detail turns to Reopen with the close in its rail, the row shows Closed and Open goes 23 → 22, the Closed tab lists it with Reopen inside the table's edge, reopen from the row, empty state. Checks the close body (`reason`, `note`, `reopen_at` as a start of day with its offset), one reopen, and the summary fetched again.

`node drive.mjs findings`: summary ("20 findings, 4 groups") and the four groups, open CVE-2021-41773, reload, get an action plan (the mock answers after about 2.5 s), unfold Vulnerability, check the detail names no agent, inference or Scoring Model, send "Should be attended earlier" with a note, close. The modal is `getByRole('dialog')`; inside it, "Why" also matches the "Why it is here" section, so the note is `getByRole('textbox', { name: 'Why', exact: true })`.

Unreachable here: the empty state (the mock always has two prioritizations; it needs a backend with none), plan errors (refused, rejected, unavailable), a Finding closed on its own (none in the fixtures; the unit tests cover it), the 409s (`finding_closed`, `finding_not_closed_by_hand`).

## Gotchas
- Fixtures `src/mocks/prioritizations-store.ts`: the newest prioritization has 20 findings in 4 groups (4, 5, 6, 5), the older one 19; group texts and Luna's plan are in Spanish whatever the UI language. CVE-2024-6387 is "New"; CVE-2024-23897 already has a plan. Mock state: localStorage `barbattack_mock_prioritizations`.
- Findings do not depend on a CMDB connection in mock mode.
- Closed by hand: localStorage `barbattack_mock_closed_findings` (empty in the fixtures). The mock's Open count is 23 minus the Findings closed by hand.
- The modal's own close button is the ✕ named "Close": match it with `exact: true`, or "Close finding" matches too.
- End state: one `POST /prioritizations/<id>/plans`, one `POST .../feedback` with verdict `should_be_earlier`, and the plan shown in the modal.
